v10a (superclass {motorcycle, bicycle, pedestrian} evidence + class

## Findings
1. Superclass evidence is nearly universal: support > 0 in 28/30
   cells (13/30 under v9). Binary detectability saturates under v10a
   and stops discriminating arms.
2. The v9 lever advantage does not survive: mean S_perc spreads
   collapse (bank0 arms within 0.24-0.30; bank1 arms equal at 0.216).
   The probe-record prediction is confirmed at protocol level: FT and
   dims do not create rendered actor evidence.
3. The lever effect relocates to class fidelity: b1_anchor scores
   fidelity 0.0 in all five cases (actor evidence is person-labeled),
   while FT-arm front-view evidence is predominantly
   motorcycle-labeled (b0_ft fidelity 1.0 on its detected non-m4
   cells). Both human reviews (far-entry person-like verdict;
   stacked-arm clear-motorcycle verdict) match this reading: the
   levers change what the actor reads as, not whether something is
   rendered.
4. m4 under v10a is a false-positive mode, not actor evidence: the
   selected view flips to the back cameras (v3/v4), fidelity is 0.0
   in every arm, and the only multi-frame tracks in the matrix
   (length 2-3) live there - consistent with the unsubtracted
   scene-pedestrian residue documented in the probe record, on a case
   whose real-track mapping is empty at both banks. Consequence: the
   v10b maneuver-relevant view restriction is a precondition for any
   v10 adoption, not an optional refinement.
5. Superclass pooling can also dilute: arms with extra person
   detections alongside the motorcycle lose a little Q_id/Q_conf
   (e.g. b0_ftdims m1 .164 -> .155). Minor relative to findings 2-4.
